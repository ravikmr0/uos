# UOS Redesign

Project: Uttarakhand Open School (UOS), Dehradun — Official Website Redesign

Build a modern, clean, government/education-style website for Uttarakhand Open School, Dehradun (uos.ac.in), a state-approved open schooling board. Replace the current outdated table-based design with a responsive, trustworthy, accessible site while keeping all the informational content intact.

Brand & Tone: Official, trustworthy, government-education aesthetic. Primary palette: deep blue/navy (#1a3c6e or similar) with saffron/orange and white accents (reflecting Indian state education branding). Clean sans-serif typography, generous whitespace, no clutter — unlike the current cramped legacy layout.

Global Elements:

Top utility bar: Home | Login | Sitemap

Logo + "Uttarakhand Open School, Dehradun®" with "(Approved and Recognised)" tagline

Main nav: Home, About Us, Courses, Affiliation, Fee Structure, Schools, AI Centers, Study Material, About State, Board Profile, Calendar, Contact Us

Sidebar/quick-links widget (Student Section): Eligibility, Admit Card, Enrollment Card, Exam Results, Student Login, Online Database, Download Section, Notices

Footer: disclaimer text, "©2018 All Rights Reserved Uttarakhand Open School, Dehradun", last-updated date, visitor counter

Homepage sections:

Hero banner — mission statement: providing secondary/senior secondary education to disadvantaged and weaker sections (SC/ST, socially/educationally/economically disadvantaged groups) via open distance learning.

"Admissions Open (Session Oct 2026)" call-to-action banner with a "Download Date Sheet" button.

Feature cards (4-grid):

Free Education for All Needy Students — free schooling up to senior secondary level for disadvantaged groups

High School Level (Class X) — eligibility: 14 years of age as on 1st August of admission year

Dual Enrollment & Part Admission — for students in regular school or already holding a certificate; up to 4 subjects, mark sheet only (no certificate)

Intermediate School Level (Class XII) — eligibility: 16 years of age as on 1st August, after qualifying entrance test

"Validity of Academic Certificates" section — explains Board certificates are registered for government employment and further education, per Board statutes.

Vocational Education section — link/callout to "Workshop on Skill Development & Vocational Training Initiatives in Uttarakhand by UOS."

About Us page: Full mission/vision content — serving school dropouts, working professionals seeking promotion, disadvantaged groups; functions as subsidiary of the Department of Education; follows NCERT/CBSE curriculum guidelines; goals include establishing study/academic centers, individualized flexible learning, and expanding vocational/continuing education.

Board Profile page: List the Board's governing authorities as a clean org-structure/accordion layout:

General Body

Governing Body

Executive Committee

Academic Committee

Finance Committee

Research Committee

Schools/Academic Centers Development Committee

Online Education Committee

Courses page: Two main course tracks with eligibility criteria clearly laid out in cards or tables:

High School (10th Class) — age 14+ as on 1 Aug

Intermediate (12th Class) — age 16+ as on 1 Aug, entrance test required

Functional requirements:

Responsive design (mobile-first), since original was IE6-era table layout

Student Login portal (placeholder auth page)

Downloadable date sheet/PDF links section

Notices/announcements page with a listing layout

Contact Us page with address, map embed placeholder, and enquiry form

Sitemap page

Do not include: the "best viewed in 1024x768 / Internet Explorer 6.0" note, the raw visitor counter styling, or table-based layouts — modernize these into clean UI equivalents (e.g., a simple stats badge instead of a visitor counter, if desired).
